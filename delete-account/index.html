Account and data deletion · Hedef 10

Developer: Tunahan Çakır · App: Hedef 10 (com.tunahancakir.hedef10)

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Hedef 10.
  2. Go to Settings › Profile (or tap your name on the Weekly challenge screen).
  3. Tap "Delete my account and data" at the bottom and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately.

If you can't open the app

Email info.tunahancakir@gmail.com with the subject "Hedef 10 data deletion" and the nickname shown on the leaderboard. After verifying the account we delete it within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted

  • Your nickname, avatar and frame choice
  • All your weekly scores and podium badges
  • Emojis you received and sent
  • Your anonymous online account

What stays

  • Game progress, records, hints and login streak on your device (uninstalling the app removes them too).
  • Reports you filed are kept for up to 90 days for abuse review, then deleted.
