Privacy Policy

Apps: Hedef 10 (com.tunahancakir.hedef10) and Mevsiminde Türkiye (com.tunahancakir.mevsiminde)
Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what data the apps above, published by Tunahan Çakır ("we"), process and how. If this English text and the Turkish version differ, the Turkish version applies.

In short

  • You don't need an account; we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress (Hedef 10) and your market list (Mevsiminde Türkiye) are stored on your device. For Hedef 10's weekly leaderboard, your nickname and score are kept online (see below).
  • The apps show ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects the data listed below.

Data that stays on your device

In Hedef 10, your board, best score and settings; in Mevsiminde Türkiye, your shopping list are kept in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot access this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it permanently.

Ads and Google AdMob

Ads are shown with Google LLC's Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). According to Google, this SDK automatically collects the following data and shares it with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

DataDescription
IP address / approximate locationMay be used to estimate the device's general location.
App interactionsSuch as app launches, ad taps and video views.
DiagnosticsApp and SDK performance (such as launch time and hang rate).
Device identifiersAndroid advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit with TLS. For how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Weekly challenge, profile and emojis (Hedef 10)

Hedef 10's weekly challenge, leaderboard, profile and emojis run on Google Firebase (Google Cloud). The app creates an anonymous online account without asking for an email or password. For this service we process:

DataWhyWho sees it
Anonymous player IDTo link your score and profile to you and prevent abuseOnly the system
The nickname, avatar and frame you chooseTo appear on the leaderboardAll players
Your weekly best scoreLeaderboard and leagues; frames for the week's top threeAll players
Emojis you send and receivePlayers cheering each other (fixed emoji set, no free text)Only the recipient
Your reportsTo review offensive names or suspected cheatingOnly us

This data is encrypted in transit with TLS and stored on Google's servers in Europe. A score played offline is stored on your device first and sent when you are back online. You won't see emojis from players you block. You can delete your online data at any time (see below).

Consent and your choices

  • Where required, the app shows Google's consent form (UMP) on first launch. You can accept or decline personalized ads; if you decline, limited or non-personalized ads may be shown.
  • You can change your choice from "Privacy options" in the app (the link appears only where it is required in your region).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Children

The apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Deleting data and your rights

In Hedef 10, Profile › "Delete my account and data" permanently deletes your nickname, avatar, all your scores, the emojis you sent and received and your anonymous account. If you can't open the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page. Write to us to exercise your rights under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and other applicable laws; for advertising data, contact Google.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date and publish it on this page.
